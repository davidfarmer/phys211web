Checkpoint 3.8.13.

Playing Catch on a Train I
🔗
Note: This is a non-relativistic physics problem.
🔗
You are riding in a box car of a train that is traveling along the tracks at \(30{m/s}\text{.}\) You are bored, so you start to play catch with your friend who is standing on the opposite side of the box car, \(5{m}\) away from you. You throw the ball at a speed of \(10{m/s}\) straight to your friend, who catches the ball; the given speed and direction are determined in your reference frame. You may ignore any vertical motion of the ball.
🔗
  1. How long does it take for the ball to reach your friend according to you?
    🔗
    🔗
  2. How long does it take for the ball to reach your friend according to an observer standing at rest on the ground? (This is a dumb question in classical physics — if the answer isn’t obvious, you’re thinking too hard!)
    🔗
    🔗
  3. How far does the ball travel according to the observer standing on the ground?
    🔗
    🔗
  4. What is the speed of the ball according to the observer standing on the ground?
