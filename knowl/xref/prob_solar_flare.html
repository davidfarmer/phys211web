Checkpoint 4.5.10.

The Earth is \(8{lt-min}\) from the Sun. An astronomer on Earth, looking through a telescope, notices the sudden appearance of a giant solar flare on the Sun’s surface. At precisely that instant (when the astronomer detects the flare), a Klingon space ship whizzes over his head at speed \(0.8c\text{,}\) heading straight for the Sun.
  1. On the facing page, construct a spacetime diagram for this situation. Label the following three events: A: Klingon ship hits Sun, B: flare occurs on Sun, and C: Klingon ship passes Earth. (Note that event B is the occurrence of the flare on the Sun, not the detection of the flare by an astronomer on Earth.)
