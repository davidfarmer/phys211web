Melting iron
  1. Use your results from Problem 7. Checkpoint 7.11.2 to estimate the typical thermal displacement for atoms in a chunk of solid iron at room temperature. (Assume a room temperature of \(22^{\circ}{C}\text{.}\)) Compare your result to the typical lattice separation for the iron atoms. Based on this result and the Lindemann criterion, explain why it is reasonable that iron is a solid at room temperature.

  2. Now, assuming that iron melts when the typical displacement is one-tenth the lattice separation (i.e., \(x_\text{ therm } \approx d/10\text{,}\) which is the Lindemann criterion), estimate the melting temperature of iron. Compare your result to the experimental value.
