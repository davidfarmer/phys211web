Problem 0.77 Tarzan.

A \(17\,\text{m}\) vine hangs vertically from a tree on one side of a \(10\,\text{m}\)-wide gorge. Tarzan wants to run toward the vine, grab ahold of it, swing over the gorge, let go of the vine, and drop vertically to the ground on the other side of the gorge. How fast must he run to make sure that he makes it across the gorge?
