Checkpoint 1.0.14. Wondrous Wedge.

A \(2{kg}\) block is placed on a frictionless wedge that is inclined at an angle of \(60^\circ\) from the horizontal as shown in Figure 1.0.15. If you release the block, it would slide down the wedge. It turns out that if you push the wedge to the right with just the right acceleration \(a\text{,}\) the block will actually remain stationary with respect to the wedge. In other words, the block will not have any vertical acceleration, though it does have the same horizontal acceleration as the wedge.
