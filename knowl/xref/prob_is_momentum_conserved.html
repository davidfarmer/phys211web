Checkpoint 5.10.20.

Are momentum and energy conserved?
🔗
A mad scientist at rest in a lab on the earth is colliding particles together to make more massive particles. She creates head-on collisions of particles with a mass of \(4{GeV/ c^2 }\) and speed \(0.6c\) to create particles at rest with a mass of \(10{GeV/ c^2 }\) (see Figure 5.10.20.)
🔗
In the rest frame of the scientist, the \(4{GeV/ c^2 }\) particles have equal and oppositely directed momenta, and the \(10{GeV/ c^2 }\) particle is at rest, so momentum is conserved.
🔗
An observer in a rocket flying over the lab at a speed \(v=0.6c\) views this experiment (see Figure 5.10.20.)
