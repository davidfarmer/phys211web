Problem 0.38 Photon Absorption.

An elementary particle has a mass of \(1125\,\text{MeV} /c^2\text{,}\) and is motionless in some reference frame. A photon with momentum \(750\,\text{MeV} /c\) strikes the particle and is absorbed, leaving an “excited” particle that is recoiling and nothing else. Determine the mass and recoil velocity of the excited particle after the interaction.
