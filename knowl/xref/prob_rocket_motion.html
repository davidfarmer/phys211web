Checkpoint 1.0.6. Rocket Motion.

\begin{equation*} y(t) = 70 + 40t + 0.3t^3 \end{equation*}
during the first moments of takeoff, where \(y\) is the height of the rocket in meters and \(t\) is the time in seconds after the rocket clears the support tower. Assuming the motion of the rocket is purely vertical, determine both the speed and the acceleration of the rocket at time \(t = 10{s}\text{.}\)
