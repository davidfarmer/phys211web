Example 10.3.2. Three oscillators, two energy units.

Write down all the microstates for a system of three oscillators and a total of two energy units, and determine the multiplicity. Solution: For microstates written as \((n_1,n_2,n_3)\text{,}\) we need to have \(n_1+n_2+n_3=2\text{,}\) so the possible microstates are
\begin{equation*} (2,0,0),(0,2,0),(0,0,2), (1,1,0),(1,0,1),(0,1,1), \end{equation*}
and the multiplicity \(\Omega=6\text{.}\)
