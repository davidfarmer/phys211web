Problem 6.7.

A 20 kg brick of lead is dropped from a height of \(5.0\,\text{m}\) above the sidewalk. It falls to the ground where it comes to rest. Assume that 60% of the mechanical energy of the brick is converted to thermal energy of the brick (the remaining energy went into thermal energy of the sidewalk and a big crack). Determine the temperature increase of the brick. Hint: you will need to calculate how many moles of lead the brick contains.
