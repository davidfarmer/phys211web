Checkpoint 1.0.67. Orbits in a Non-Keplerian Solar System, Part I.

Suppose that the gravitational force of attraction depended not on \(1/r^2\text{,}\) but rather was proportional to the distance between the two masses (like the force due to a stretched spring). In a planetary system that felt this different form of gravity, what would be the relationship between the period of a planet and its orbital radius? Assume circular orbits.
