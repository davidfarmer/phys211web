The idea of “diminishing returns” says that while the entropy does increase with increasing thermal energy, the slope is decreasing (see Figure 10.6.1). The Einstein solid multiplicity, like most materials, shows this behavior. Here is how to see it:
  1. For an Einstein solid with 10 oscillators and 5 energy units, calculate how much the entropy increases, i.e. \(\Delta S\text{,}\) if you add one more energy unit (you may leave your answer in terms of \(k_B\)).
